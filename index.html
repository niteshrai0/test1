PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Configuration beats repeated customisation.

When every client looks different, the instinct is to keep building exceptions. The more scalable question is whether those differences can become rules, states and reusable primitives.

THE CONTROL LAYER

Same platform. Different partner behaviour.

Once data enters the platform, partner configuration and business rules determine how a journey behaves—what products are relevant, what data is required, which validations apply and how the workflow should branch. My work increasingly moved from individual integrations into these broader enterprise and product requirements.

AIRe · INSIDE THE SAME SYSTEM

The workflow knew how to execute. Could it also help someone decide?

Customer context+Loan context+Product context+Eligibility / constraints→AIRe→Ranked recommendations→Human decision

I worked on the problem definition, decision context, recommendation workflow, requirements, business constraints, experience, engineering collaboration and validation. AIRe evolved from a single recommendation toward a shortlist for more complex assisted journeys. The intent was decision support—not replacing the salesperson who owns the customer conversation.
